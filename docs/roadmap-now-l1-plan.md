#### E1 acceptance and panel derivation

See the [settled E1 acceptance table](#e1-acceptance-bar) and [whole-round false-miss projections](#whole-round-estimates-for-e1).

For each model, let X and Y be independent Binomial(5, p) counts for the two installs. The per-model miss probability is one minus the sum of their joint probabilities over pairs that meet the settled pooled and per-install cutoffs. Use p = 90% for Haiku and 95% for Sonnet and Opus at nominal rates, 85% and 90% respectively at pessimistic rates, and 70% to estimate power when both installs have degraded. These are sensitivity assumptions, not measured pass rates for the new checks.

These are pre-freeze projections. For each model, sum the joint binomial mass for two five-run installs that meet both floors and the pooled cutoff. Multiply the resulting pass probabilities across the model’s core screens. Include illustrative regression false misses of 3–6% per model. For Round 2, the routing proxies add 3.2% on Haiku and 0.5% each on Sonnet and Opus for the new positives, plus routing-margin ranges of 9–10%, 0–0.9% and 0–0.1%, respectively. A core screen is the joint named outcome for one case and model. The baseline replays, probes, resumed CV regression and report-only multiple-posting case add no core screens. The Round 2 regression proxy excludes the new CV control until it can be calibrated.
